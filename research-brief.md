# HOW NEST AFRICA AI INNOVATION LAB USES DATA FOR IMPACT IN AFRICA

## 1. Who are they?

Nest Africa AI Innovation Lab is a Nigerian non-profit research and innovation organization building artificial intelligence and climate intelligence solutions for Africa. Its mission is to use locally relevant data, research and AI technology to address challenges affecting African communities. Its work focuses on climate resilience, responsible AI, agriculture and sustainable development.

## 2. What data do they use?

Nest Africa uses local climate data, weather information, rainfall patterns, seasonal forecasts, environmental information and community knowledge. It combines these with artificial intelligence and climate models to develop practical solutions. One of its major projects is AyeMole AI, a climate intelligence platform that provides localized weather forecasts, early warnings, climate information and agricultural insights. It also supports communication in African languages, making climate information easier for communities to understand.

## 3. What changed because of it?

Through its climate AI initiatives, Nest Africa is helping communities access useful climate information and understand environmental risks. Its official website reports over 500 active users, engagement with more than 20 farmer communities and over 1,500 people reached through field engagement. These efforts support climate awareness and help farmers and communities make more informed decisions about weather-related challenges.

## Conclusion

Nest Africa AI Innovation Lab demonstrates how data and artificial intelligence can be used to solve real-world problems in Africa. By combining climate information, local knowledge and AI technology, the organization is helping build a more informed and climate-resilient future for African communities.
